This release is the J push one step short of its milestone: the parts of J that a program is written with, not only the primitives it computes with. Locales, control words, the fold conjunctions, the foreign families that compute, and exact arithmetic through matrix inversion and the primes. 0.5.0 is reserved for the last measured families; docs/coverage.md names each of them with the reason it waits.

Programs now have somewhere to live. Locales are named namespaces for globals (`V_alpha_ =. 5`, `f_base_ 3`), with the `18!:` foreigns to create, list and read them, indirect locatives (`g__n`) that reach a locale a noun names, and multiple assignment (`'a b' =. 1 2`). Control flow gained `goto_name.` and `label_name.`, and `throw.` with `catcht.` to leave a definition and every caller between it and the catcher. Tacit definition (`13 :`) reads an explicit body and answers the train it means; `{{)n` is the direct-definition spelling of a noun; a definition's body may be written on the lines below it; and a modifier's body may derive a modifier again.

Names and trains agree with the reference in the corners a program actually reaches: a named noun in a fork's tine, a name that holds a modifier, a verb or modifier sentence displaying itself, the capped fork keeping the spelling it was written with, an explicit modifier's noun operand answering to both of its names, and an undefined name standing as the verb a train needs. The `5!:` representations draw every verb shape in atomic and linear form, and the linear form brackets a hook, holds a modifier off the word before it, and never takes a constant verb apart, exactly where the reference does.

The fold family arrived whole: `u F.. v`, `u F.: v`, `u F:. v` and `u F:: v`, folding forward or backward, keeping every step or only the last, with `Z:` to stop early. Beside it: `u"v` with a verb on the right lending its ranks, gerunds under the cut and under the rank conjunction, the gerund amend's monadic valence, `L:_` and `S:_` as the infinite levels, `!.` as a fill on every verb the language gives one to, per-axis cut frets from a boxed left argument, the `$.` storage forms, and `__:` for the constant verb of the infinity below.

The foreigns that only compute are in. `3!:1` and `3!:3` write an array as the bytes the reference writes, extended and rational numbers included, byte for byte against the interpreter. `4!:0` and `4!:55` read the name table, `5!:1`, `5!:2` and `5!:5` the representations, `8!:0` to `8!:2` the formats, `9!:10`, `9!:11`, `9!:18` and `9!:19` the print precision and comparison tolerance, and `128!:3` the CRC-32 of a byte string.

Arithmetic keeps its exactness further. `%.` stays rational or extended wherever the inverse is exact, solves a complex system as the real system of twice the size, reads an atom as a reciprocal and an empty system as the zero the reference gives, and divides a right-hand side of any rank. The prime count `_1 p:` is computed sublinearly and reaches the reference's own bound of 2^31; `4 p:` answers one past the machine word; `x p: y` follows the measured rule for a `y` that is not whole; `_2 x:` is the alternating quotient; `#.` and `#:` read complex numbers; the binomial holds at every magnitude; and the empty frame of every arithmetic verb, the polynomial family included, carries the type the reference gives it.

Behind the release is the agreement loop: generated expressions run against jconsole in sweeps of fifty thousand, every disagreement classified as a fix, a pinned divergence with both answers recorded and the reason, or a registered gap, with zero panics and zero internal errors allowed through. The grammar that measured 0.4.0 now agrees on 99.9% of what it draws; a wider grammar that draws explicit definitions, control words, gerunds, folds and foreigns agrees on 99.2% and is the measure 0.5.0 will be held to. A sweep now survives the sentence that kills the runner, resumes from its journal, and reports raw and adjusted agreement side by side.

Nothing in the public API is displaced. The Python, Rust and C surfaces are unchanged; every addition is language coverage behind the same string.
